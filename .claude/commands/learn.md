Use the `teach` skill and follow it completely — probe first (quiz me to find the edge of what I know, and ask what I actually want out of this), then plan and show me the dependency map, wait for my go-ahead, then teach node by node.

Mirror every teaching message into the session log with `mcp__learn__log`, and use `visualize` whenever a picture genuinely carries the idea better than prose.
